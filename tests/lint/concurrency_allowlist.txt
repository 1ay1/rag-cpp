# rag-cpp — concurrency allowlist (checked by agentty's concurrency_banlist_rag).
#
# rag-cpp owns no thread pool: every parallel region runs on the
# rag::util::Executor the host installs. Entries are the standalone fallback
# executor in util/parallel.hpp (the one place a thread is named) and the
# locks, atomics and per-thread scratch buffers that keep the index, caches
# and plugins safe under the host's threads.
